# Privacy Policy of Bestemmiometro
Last Updated: 29/08/2026

*With "Bestemmiometro" we refer to the Discord bot @Bestemmiometro#8967 exclusively.*
*With "us" we refer to Bestemmiometro's developer team and collaborators.*
*With "our services" we refer to Bestemmiometro, to its Dashboard and to its API.*

# What data do we store?
We store data that from Discord and includes user IDs, server IDs, custom swears and other data like a server's swears number or the settings.

# Why do we need this data?
We need this data to give users a better experience during the use of our services.

# How do we use the data?
We use the data to offer customized features of our services (like custom swears) or to save server's swears' count.

# Do we share your data with other services?
No, our data stays in our databases only and is not shared with third parties. We make backups periodically for disaster recovery purposes. These backups are uploaded to secure cloud storage services (Google Drive). Before being uploaded, the backups are encrypted through client-side asymmetric encryption methods. No cloud storage service can read the backups. 

# Is your data secure?
Yes: the data stored on our server is protected by various security measures, including but not limited to database encryption and SSH connection to make sure the data is only accessible by our services. The backups stored on the cloud storage services are protected through procedures which are described above.

# How can a user contact us if they have questions about their data?
A user can contact us by joining [Bestemmiometro's official support server](https://discord.gg/TFubcWSM4h9) or by sending a friend request to @thisisanto.

# How long do we keep your data?
The data is stored in our database until the elimination is requested. The backups are automatically deleted when they reach 30 days of age.

# How can I delete my data?
The data associated with a server (ID, number of swears, custom swears, leaderboard, etc.) can be deleted by its owner through the /impostazioni command of Bestemmiometro.
The data associated with an user (ID, number of swears, etc.) can be deleted by requesting deletion in [Bestemmiometro's support server](https://discord.gg/TFubcWSM4h9) or by contacting one of the bot owners: @thisisanto, @clauz2942, @fleny.
Any and all deleted data is excluded from future backups.
For further information, please join [Bestemmiometro's support server](https://discord.gg/TFubcWSM4h9).
